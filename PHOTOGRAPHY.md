## Processing

Downloaded directly from the Pexels image CDN with TLS verification. Converted to WebP at quality 82 with metadata removed, resized and cropped for the layout. Hero is 1920 × 1080; service images are 1100 × 760; planning image is 1100 × 1000. No material content was added or changed. Responsive derivatives are also available: `hero-960.webp` and `residential-550.webp`, `commercial-550.webp`, `selective-550.webp`, `planning-550.webp`.
